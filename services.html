Garage Floor Epoxy

Garage floor epoxy seals a dusty, stained concrete slab with a full flake or solid-color coating. It solves peeling store-bought coatings, oil stains that will not come out and the fine concrete dust that settles on everything in the garage.

It suits anyone who parks in the garage or uses it as a gym, shop or storage room. Left alone, bare concrete keeps soaking up oil and shedding dust, and a failing coating only peels further each summer.

Call us today for garage floor epoxy in Fontana. Read more about garage floor epoxy.

Polyaspartic Floor Coatings

A polyaspartic floor coating is a fast-curing, UV-stable coating that is often installed in a single day. It solves the yellowing that standard epoxy shows in direct sun and gets the garage back in use quickly.

It is the right pick for garages that sit with the door open and for anyone who cannot be without the garage for a week. An epoxy-only topcoat in a sunny garage slowly ambers and cannot be brought back.

Call us today for polyaspartic floor coatings in Fontana. Read more about polyaspartic floor coatings.

Metallic Epoxy Floors

Metallic epoxy flooring is a decorative coating with pigments worked by hand into wet resin for a marbled, glossy look. It turns plain concrete in a garage, gym, office or showroom into a one-of-a-kind floor.

It suits homeowners and businesses who want a showpiece floor. Laid over a slab that was not ground flat, a metallic floor shows every dip and crack, so prep is everything.

Call us today for metallic epoxy floors in Fontana. Read more about metallic epoxy floors.

Commercial & Warehouse Epoxy

Commercial epoxy flooring protects warehouse, shop and kitchen floors with high-build coatings, joint filling and safety markings. It solves concrete dusting, chipped joints, chemical stains and faded lanes.

It suits distribution centers, auto shops, food businesses and landlords preparing a space for a new tenant. Ignored, chipped joints grow and dusting concrete keeps coating product and racking.

Call us today for commercial and warehouse epoxy in Fontana. Read more about commercial and warehouse epoxy.
